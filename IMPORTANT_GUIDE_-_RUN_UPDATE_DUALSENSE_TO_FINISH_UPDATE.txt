5. Confirm the update in UPDATE_DUALSENSE.exe.
6. Follow the BeamNG installation tutorial that opens after the update.
7. Install the matching Enhanced_PS5_DualSense_Haptics_Vx.xx.zip in your BeamNG mods folder without extracting it.
